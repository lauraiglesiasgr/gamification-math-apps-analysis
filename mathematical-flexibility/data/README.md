# Aggregate inputs

These CSV files are manual transcriptions of thesis Tables 4.1 and 4.2, not original participant data. Decimal points are used. The score range is 0–20. Strategy counts are criterion ratings (15 participants × 6 criteria per game); totals include not-observable ratings.

These inputs support regeneration of the portfolio figures. No diagnostic information, participant identifiers or individual observations are supplied. They do not reproduce the original statistical analysis.
